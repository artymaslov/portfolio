About

I'm Artem Maslov, an AI Art Director and Creative Technologist based in Edmonton, Canada.

I work where art direction meets technology. Over 9 years I've moved from concept art for Snapchat lenses to leading AR activations, Apple Vision Pro prototypes and large-scale installations, and now to AI-driven production.

Clients include Dior, Universal Pictures, Paramount Animation, Cartier, Snapchat, L'Oréal, Amazon and Visit Dubai. Previously Art Director at Tactical (Rotterdam), founder of ARNI Studio, and Senior Concept Artist at Snap Inc. I also teach visual assets for games at NAIT.
