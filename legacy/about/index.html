I’m Artem Maslov, an AI art director and creative technologist in Edmonton. For nine years I made images for brands, studios and platforms: concept art at Snap, my own studio ARNI, art direction at Tactical in Rotterdam.

Then a second instrument arrived. Today I write code, build generative pipelines, and approach creative problems the way a lab does — a question, a test, a measurement. This site holds both versions of me.
