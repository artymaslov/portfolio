Made

A set of speculative visuals and motion concepts for Lexus, commissioned by a Middle Eastern agency as internal R&D.

Needed

The agency wanted to see how AI could change brand storytelling: faster ideation and new pipelines, without diluting a premium brand.

Worth

Tested workflows and a body of concept visuals that show where future Lexus campaigns could go, and how fast they could get there.

The problem

Generative tools move faster than brand pipelines. The question was whether art direction, technology and automation could work together to strengthen a luxury brand’s identity rather than flatten it into generic AI imagery.

What I built

Node-based workflows in Weavy tied image generation, video models and 3D together into one repeatable pipeline. Stills set the look, video models carried it into motion, and Blender kept form and product accurate.
