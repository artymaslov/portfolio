overview

Part of the global marketing campaign for Jurassic World: Rebirth (2025): an immersive Snapchat AR filter with cinematic-quality interaction, made with Pretty Big Monster.

my part

I modeled the hero helmet and suit. The suit was built in Marvelous Designer for natural fabric behaviour and realistic folds; the helmet was optimised for real-time mobile AR without losing fidelity. I also helped with rigging and animation so the motion reacts smoothly to the user.
