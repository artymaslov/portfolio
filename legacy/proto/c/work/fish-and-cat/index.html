Fish and Cat

A short film made entirely with AI: no camera, no keyframes.

Project hero 16:9

What it is

Made. A short narrative video produced with a fully AI-driven workflow.

Needed. To find out how far generative tools can carry a coherent story without filming or manual animation.

Worth. A finished piece and a tested workflow where AI is the medium for directing, editing and production, not a helper.

The problem

AI video still breaks stories: characters drift between shots, scenes don’t match, and motion fights the edit.

What I built

Nano Banana Pro held scene consistency and style; Seedance 1.5 Pro handled motion. Directing happened in the prompts, the edit happened between generations.
