Fish and Cat

A short film made entirely with AI: no camera, no keyframes.

typeSelf-initiated
roleDirector, AI Artist
formatShort video
year[Year]

What it is

A short narrative video produced with a fully AI-driven workflow.

Why it existed

To find out how far generative tools can carry a coherent story without filming or manual animation.

What it was worth

A finished piece and a tested workflow where AI is the core medium for directing, editing and production, not just a helper.

01

Problem

AI video still breaks stories: characters drift between shots, scenes don’t match, and motion fights the edit.

02

Solution

Nano Banana Pro held scene consistency and style; Seedance 1.5 Pro handled motion. Directing happened in the prompts, the edit happened between generations.
