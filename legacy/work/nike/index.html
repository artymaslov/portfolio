Nike — AI R&D

Prototyping the future of brand storytelling with generative tools.

clientNike (via [agency name])
roleArt Director, AI Artist
typeInternal R&D
year[Year]

What it is

Speculative visuals and motion concepts for Nike, built as an internal R&D exploration for a Middle Eastern agency.

Why it existed

A look at how generative AI could speed up ideation and give future campaigns dynamic, repeatable creative pipelines.

What it was worth

A tested mix of node-based AI, 3D and prompt engineering, plus concept visuals the agency could use to pitch the approach.

01

Problem

Brand storytelling still runs on long production cycles. The research asked how AI could accelerate ideation while keeping the art direction recognisably Nike.

02

Solution

A pipeline that combined node-based generation in ComfyUI, Adobe Firefly and 3D in Blender, so concepts could move from sketch to motion test quickly.
